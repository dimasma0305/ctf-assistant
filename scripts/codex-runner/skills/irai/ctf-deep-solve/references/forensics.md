# Forensics CTFs

Preserve the original and record hashes. Build a timeline and provenance model before extracting isolated curiosities. Identify container/file-system/packet/memory formats and tool versions.

Correlate metadata, filesystem state, logs, packet streams, process memory, document internals, deleted content, and time zones. Treat carving and scanner hits as candidates until their surrounding structure and chronology agree. Decode layers with scripts that preserve intermediate outputs and hashes.

Proof is a defensible chain from the supplied evidence to the recovered flag or answer, including offsets, stream/object identifiers, timestamps, and decoding steps. A string search hit without provenance can be a decoy.
