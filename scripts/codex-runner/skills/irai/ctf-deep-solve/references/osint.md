# OSINT CTFs

Define the entity, time window, required answer, and permitted public sources. Separate direct source facts from identity or location inference. Preserve URLs, timestamps, archive snapshots, and the exact observable that links records.

Use independent corroboration for identity, ownership, or geolocation claims when the challenge requires it. Do not contact people, authenticate to accounts, purchase data, or expose private information as part of a solve unless the challenge rules and user explicitly authorize the action.

For multi-source or historical investigations, use the compact [OSINT framework cheatsheet](osint-cheatsheet.md) to track entity links, event and capture dates, alternative explanations, and the exact answer gate.

For person or account attribution, use [person OSINT](person-osint.md) to resolve direct profile links, publisher credits, and namesakes before drawing biographical conclusions.
