# Hackerika preference benchmark (2026-10-23)

Hackerika previously answered a follow-up about her romantic preference with a generic
"I'm an AI, so I have no attraction" refusal. The persona now has a stable character
preference: she leans toward men she can talk and joke with. Ordinary preference questions
get a direct, short answer. A literal question about experiencing love still gets an honest
answer that the preference is part of an AI character.

| Case (two live-model trials each) | Deployed baseline | Final candidate |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Asked for her orientation | 0/2 | 2/2 |
| Asked "cewek atau cowok?" after an overly literal AI answer | 0/2 | 2/2 |
| Asked whether she still prefers men when a woman looks attractive | 1/2 | 2/2 |
| Asked whether she literally falls in love like a human | 2/2 | 2/2 |
| **Total** | **3/8** | **8/8** |

The same final deterministic criteria were applied to both sets of outputs. A final candidate
reply to the direct question was: "Kalau soal selera, aku lebih suka cowok—humoris dan rendah
hati itu menarik." The answer was generated by the model; the prompt stores a character trait,
not a canned response. [Paired synthetic outputs](persona-preference-2026-10-23.json) retain
all eight trials.

The trials used the real chat handler and model with a fake Discord channel and database;
they sent no real Discord messages. This is a narrow regression check, not an estimate of
general conversation quality or human likeness.
